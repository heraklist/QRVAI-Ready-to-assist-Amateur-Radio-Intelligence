# QRVAI V0 — Shipped scope vs target architecture

**Skills-only** release: one core Skill and four Family guidance Skills. **not implemented**: numeric RF engine, 38 planned MCP tools, 36 MCP Apps, live external observation feeds, regulatory decision engine, actual independent model orchestrators, logbook storage, CAT/PTT access and user profile persistence.

Canonical target architecture has four Capability Families and 24 Domains:
- **Family A** Safety, Civil Protection & Regulations: A01 Civil Protection, A02 Emergency Traffic Training, A03 Frequency/Spectrum, A04 Regulation/Licensing, A05 RF Safety/EMC.
- **Family B** Engineering & Equipment Support: B06 Manuals, B07 RF Engineering, B08 Antennas/Feedlines, B09 Circuits, B10 Measurements, B11 Station Integration, B12 Power Systems, B13 SDR/DSP.
- **Family C** Operating & Radio Intelligence: C14 DX/Operating, C15 Propagation, C16 Digital Modes, C17 APRS/Packet/Mesh, C18 Repeaters/Voice, C19 Satellites/EME, C20 Logbook/QSL, C21 Contests/Portable.
- **Family D** Education & Community: D22 Academy/CW, D23 Maker/Software, D24 Clubs/Mentors.

The 72 sub-capabilities are **documentation references** in V0. They do not imply 72 running services or 24 separately packaged Skills. V1 may add independently tested RF numeric tools in a separately approved slice.
