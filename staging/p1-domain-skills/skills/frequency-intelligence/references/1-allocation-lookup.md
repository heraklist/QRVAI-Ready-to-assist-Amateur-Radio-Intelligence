# SS-A03-1 — Allocation Lookup

**Owner:** SK-A03
**Responsibility:** retrieve effective national frequency allocation and its legal status
**Required input:** Frequency_Hz, Greek/other national jurisdiction, requested date, edition/ID of legal instrument and verified amendment, exact allocation annex/footnote, reviewer state.
**Procedure:** Identify EETT national-allocation candidate instrument from the source survey, then demand operative original text, amendments/FEK, effective dates and applicability before describing binding allocation. Keep administrative indexes distinct from instruments and footnotes. Do not transform an amateur service allocation into operator permission.
**Output:** Source-layered citation candidate with checked and effective times, reviewed-state, relevant frequency interval, missing exceptions and INDETERMINATE if no verified document; no invented legal text.
**Error/stop:** No final legal privileges, no ALLOWED from a URL/snippet or EETT index alone, no automatic transmission; an unresolved date/exception/version returns SOURCE_UNVERIFIED.

**Provenance:** Source entrypoints under `docs/research/2026-10-17-qrvai-p1-2-family-a-authority-survey.md` are candidates, not already reviewed operative rules. USER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED ≠ state-authorized. No live source API or verified frequency access.
