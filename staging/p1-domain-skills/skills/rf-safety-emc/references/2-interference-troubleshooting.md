# SS-A05-2 — Interference Troubleshooting

**Owner:** SK-A05
**Responsibility:** systematically isolate RFI symptom patterns without unsafe procedures
**Required input:** Observed or synthetic RFI symptoms, affected receiver/equipment and times, safe disconnect/low-risk observational steps, known/unknown operating context.
**Procedure:** Classify symptom patterns from non-invasive observations: when the interference occurs, affected bands and devices, common-mode hypotheses, lawful/authorized receive-only checks and a table of changes. Ask technician for energized equipment work; never advise bypassing safety earth, disconnecting protective grounding, destructive modification or causing deliberate interference.
**Output:** Non-invasive troubleshooting decision tree with evidence vs inference, false positives, safe next observation and qualified-professional referral.
**Error/stop:** No mains repairs, power supply disassembly, high-voltage component probing, intentionally generated interference or radio TX tests without separate authority.

**Risk and source:** No live measurements, no guaranteed RF safety and no qualified installation certification. No mains repairs or CAT/PTT/TX action; use official qualified reviewers for real compliance.
