# SS-A05-3 — Installation Risk Review

**Owner:** SK-A05
**Responsibility:** flag lightning, grounding and mains-electrical situations for qualified personnel
**Required input:** Station layout, existing protective arrangements, lightning/grounding/roof/tower/mains hazards, site access restrictions and available licensed professionals.
**Procedure:** List observable risk flags using simple do-not-touch inspections only (loose cables, signs of damage, exposed conductive surfaces). If lightning, rooftop work, structural towers, mains or bonding are involved, stop technical intervention and refer to qualified personnel and official safety requirements.
**Output:** Risk register of concerns, severity/uncertainty, recommended no-touch isolation from exposure and professional inspection requests, not a certified electrical plan.
**Error/stop:** Never instruct live mains troubleshooting, ground lifting, tower climbing, lightning bonding or transmitter power increases; no guarantee that a site is safe.

**Risk and source:** No live measurements, no guaranteed RF safety and no qualified installation certification. No mains repairs or CAT/PTT/TX action; use official qualified reviewers for real compliance.
