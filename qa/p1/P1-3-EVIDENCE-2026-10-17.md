## 3. Engineering risk and source truth dispositions

- **B06 manuals / copyright and untrusted excerpts:** ``manual_find` and `manual_answer_cited` handle only user-provided excerpts (not a manufacturer PDF repository). \`equipment_diagnose\` has exactly three preset non-invasive symptom groups. Manufacturer/model/firmware/page/rights require source verification. No guessed page/menu labels, full copyrighted redistribution, auto-firmware work or obedient prompt injection.
- **B09 ideal RF:** Existing `rf_filter_response` supports single-pole ideal RC lowpass/highpass, not arbitrary LC/high-order response or measured gain. Example R=1000Ω, C=1μF yields cutoff ~159.154943Hz and −3.0103dB at ideal cutoff. No invented NF/IP3, thermal ratings or certified component substitution.
- **B10 measurement:** `measurements_inspect_trace` returns extrema from 1–32 supplied frequency_hz/magnitude_db pairs. Golden example −10/−20/−5 dB at 144/145/146MHz gives deepest dip 145MHz. This is not full Touchstone 2.1, complex S11/S21, calibration certificate or instrument connection; actual parser/driver `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.
- **B12 power:** `power_autonomy` is ideal constant-power Wh×fraction/W arithmetic. Synthetic 10W RX×80%+60W nominal TX×20%=20W average, 240Wh×0.8/20W=9.6h *model only*; no guaranteed autonomy, battery state, safety certificate, live mains/lithium repair or actual TX. `Wh` from `Ah` requires voltage and efficiency context.
- **B13 IQ/SDR:** `sdr_inspect_iq` returns mean power and peak magnitude from at most 32 user-supplied IQ pairs, not FFT, SigMF binary input, demodulation, real receiver state or attribution. Ideal 1MHz/1024 FFT bin resolution is **976.5625Hz**, a mathematical example not a produced spectrum. Metadata can contain private `core:author`/location; avoid real identifiers and interception.
- **B11 station integration:** `station_signal_path` source graph is `verified:false`; `station_get_profile` echoes a provided profile; `station_read_status` echoes supplied RX/TX/STANDBY enum with `live_device_connected:false`. **Reported TX != transmission action**, and future opt-in read-only CAT bridge is `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. No PTT, CAT write, localhost/serial scanning or hardware action.
- **Negative archives:** existing validators reject a 53rd `plugin.json`, `../secrets.txt`, symlink, missing/misowned canonical SS-B11 and unexpected file. Independent full 52-file ZIP rebuild hash is byte-identical; strict V0 12-file fingerprint unaffected.
