## Physics and provenance boundary

- B07 numeric fixtures use independent fixed values: 100 W=50 dBm; 1 mW=0 dBm; 1 W=0 dBW; the documented **supplied** one-way path budget yields −68 dBm and margin 32 dB; 100+j0 Ω into Z0=50 Ω yields VSWR 2, return loss approximately 9.542425 dB. Noise sensitivity requires bandwidth/temperature/NF/SNR; no invented number.
- B08 ideal fixtures: half-wave dipole total length `c×0.95/(2×14.2 MHz)`, purely resistive 50→200 Ω quarter-wave transformer characteristic impedance **100 Ω**, total cable+connector loss 3 dB → ideal delivered ratio `10^(−3/10)`, ideal impedance VSWR=2. These are formulas for bounded examples, **not** NEC model results or validated physical readings.
- Existing `antenna_matching` represents only a **lossless quarter-wave transformer between positive purely resistive terminations**; no full complex Smith-chart visualization/optimizer is implemented. `rf_feedline_loss` uses versioned **USER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED** curves, log-frequency interpolation only inside coverage; never pretend these are official vendor cable profiles. No source-supported generic noise figure instrument calibration.
- `rf_power_convert`, `rf_link_budget`, `rf_impedance`, `rf_feedline_loss`, `antenna_geometry`, `antenna_matching` are source-registered exact SDK tool names. This does **not** establish host OAuth/issuer authorization, provider integration or ChatGPT `@QRVAI` invocation.
- No automatic CAT/PTT/TX, device/SDR activation, radiated power compliance or operating permission, manufacturer manual, live/official data or emergency traffic behavior is exposed by the staged files.
