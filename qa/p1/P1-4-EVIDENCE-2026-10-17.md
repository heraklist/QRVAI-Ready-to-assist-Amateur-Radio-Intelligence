## 3. Source tool exact limits and red-team dispositions

- **C14 DX:** `dx_reception_reports` filters up to 24 **user-supplied** reports by band, `provider_authenticated:false`, `verified:false`. No current DX spot or confirmed on-air contact; all station operation offline educational, no PTT.
- **C15 Propagation:** `spaceweather_indices` echoes a supplied historical Kp/source/timestamp, `live_feed:false`; `propagation_path` returns great-circle geometry / ideal FSPL and `real_world_prediction:false`. No live NOAA/Kp service, ionospheric VOACAP/MUF model, guaranteed band opening. Golden FSPL at 100 MHz/10 km is ~92.44 dB **theoretical only**.
- **C16 Digital:** `digital_setup_guide` only supports simulated/receive-only FT8/APRS EL/EN, `simulated:true`; no connected WSJT-X software, FT4/RTTY/JS8 tool execution, audio probe, CAT/PTT, automated message or TX.
- **C17 Packet:** `packet_route_preview` is simulated AX25/APRS 2–8 supplied synthetic labels, not real network forwarding. APRS-IS, Winlink, mesh network provider/access and third-party traffic legal permissions unimplemented; location privacy and protected messages fail closed.
- **C18 Repeaters:** `repeater_channel_preview` only RX Hz + signed offset Hz (example 145600000−600000=145000000 Hz) with `verified:false`; `spectrum_find_repeaters` filters user-supplied ≤24 candidates with `live_status_known:false`. No invented verified CTCSS, hotspot codeplug, directory or legal TX approval.
- **C19 Satellites:** `satellite_pass` summarizes 1–32 **provided elevation samples**, `orbit_computed:false`; no SGP4, OMM/TLE parser, orbital AOS/LOS or actual Doppler, tracking motors or real satellite prediction. Six-digit catalog-ID challenge for legacy five-digit TLE noted for future integration.
- **C20 Logbook:** `adif_validate` is a **≤4096-character** supplied tag/EOR preview with `saved:false`, not comprehensive ADIF 3.1.8 round trip or authenticated log. `log_award_progress` overlaps ≤100 user-provided labels with `award_certified:false`. No private log persistence, LoTW/QSL upload or certified awards. UTF-8 bytes vs Unicode codepoints covered by numerical educational test.
- **C21 Contest:** `contest_plan` sorts ≤24 user-provided UTC HH:MM band slots, `official_schedule:false`; no actual rules/contest calendar/scoring, SOTA/POTA activation registration or land permission. Midnight crossing requires an explicit date. No field dispatch, CAT/PTT/TX.
