# SK-D22 — Radio Academy & CW

**Trigger:** User wants progressive radio theory, text-only CW learning or an explained original radio knowledge exercise.
**Required context:** Learner level, language el/en, selected hobby topic or academy_catalog module, accessible lesson format, optional synthetic answer and no identifiable learner data. Do not ask for licence, examination region or country to teach common hobby fundamentals.
**Output contract:** Use academy_catalog to discover nine global modules, each with three EN/EL lessons. Explain hobby topic with assumptions, source edition when verified, safe self-check and TRAINING_ONLY; academy_exercise offers only rf_power or antenna_basics fixed bilingual questions with an optional bounded answer index, never official exam grading. Theory CW example PARIS WPM (1.2/WPM dot seconds) is an IDEAL_MODEL, not measured keying or hearing performance.
**Refusal/stop condition:** No official exam certification, no unauthorized reproduced state examination question bank or fabricated Greek authority, no disclosure of private student/exam results, no on-air transmission, CAT/PTT or account writes. CW audio NOT_IMPLEMENTED and true CW scoring/hearing decoder NOT_IMPLEMENTED. No national licensing or exam curriculum belongs in the core Academy.

**Tool boundary:** academy_catalog is a source-registered international offline curriculum index; academy_exercise is a separate fixed-topic static tool for rf_power and antenna_basics in el/en only. Neither implements national licence-exam prep, CW audio, adaptive learner accounts or certified marks. No personal learner tracking. Original synthetic examples only.
